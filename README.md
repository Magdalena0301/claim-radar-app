# Claim Radar — live demo

Open the app: **https://magdalena0301.github.io/claim-radar-app/**

Marketing picks the claim it wants on the pack. Product development clicks ingredients into the mix and sees, live, whether the claim is allowed under Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 — before anything is made.

This repository holds only the deployed app file. Team 10, Digital Business and Information Systems, VU Amsterdam 2026.
Thresholds from the Annex to Reg. 1924/2006; energy factors from Reg. 1169/2011 Annex XIV. Values are for orientation, not a legal label declaration.
